# Ellustar AI Business Consultant
## Overview
Ellustar AI Business Consultant is an AI-powered business advisor built with Google Gemini. It helps entrepreneurs, freelancers, and small businesses receive personalized business guidance, marketing ideas, growth strategies, and productivity recommendations.
## Features
- AI-powered business consulting
- Marketing strategy recommendations
- Business growth insights
- Productivity and planning assistance
- Gemini-powered conversations
## Technology Stack
- Google Gemini API
- Google AI Studio
- HTML
 - CSS
- JavaScript
